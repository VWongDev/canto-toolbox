Canto Toolbox shows Chinese word definitions when you hover over characters on any webpage. Definitions appear in both Mandarin (with Pinyin) and Cantonese (with Jyutping). For each character, a radical breakdown is shown when available: radical, structure (IDS decomposition), and etymology hints (pictographic, ideographic, or pictophonetic). You can select multiple characters to look up whole phrases. Chinese inside images works too: hover a screenshot, comic panel, menu or sign, click the badge in its corner, and the text it holds becomes hoverable in place — recognised on your own machine, with nothing uploaded. The extension works offline using local dictionary data, tracks your most frequently looked-up words, supports simplified and traditional characters, and follows your system theme (including dark mode). Useful for reading Chinese online, whether you're learning Mandarin, Cantonese, or both.
